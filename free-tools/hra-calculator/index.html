HRA Calculator — FAQ

Who can claim HRA exemption?
Any salaried employee who receives HRA as part of their salary and actually pays rent for residential accommodation can claim HRA exemption u/s 10(13A). Self-employed individuals cannot claim HRA but can claim rent deduction u/s 80GG (up to ₹5,000/month).
Which cities are considered metro for HRA?
Metro cities for HRA purposes: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, and Chennai only. For these cities, the exemption limit is 50% of Basic+DA. For all other cities (including Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad), the limit is 40% of Basic+DA.
Can I claim HRA if I own a house?
Yes, but only if you live in a rented house in a different city than your owned property. If you live in your own house and pay rent to a spouse/parent, the claim may be disallowed by the IT department.
What if rent is paid to a family member?
Rent paid to parents is allowed if they declare it as income in their ITR. Rent paid to spouse is generally not allowed. You need a valid rent agreement and rent receipts as proof.
