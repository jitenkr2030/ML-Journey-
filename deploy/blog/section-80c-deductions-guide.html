Section 80C Deductions Complete List 2026-27 — Maximize Tax Savings

January 13, 20267 min readBy ReconcileAI Team

Section 80C is the most popular tax-saving provision in India. It allows deductions up to ₹1,50,000 from your taxable income for specified investments and expenses. This guide covers every eligible investment, expense, and strategy to maximize your 80C savings for FY 2026-27.

Section 80C Deduction Limit

Maximum Deduction
Section 80C: Up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year
Section 80CCD(1B) NPS: Additional ₹50,000 (over and above 80C)
Total tax-saving potential: ₹2,00,000 per year

Best 80C Strategy by Income Level

Income: ₹5-8 Lakh

  • ELSS Mutual Funds: ₹1,50,000 (best returns + shortest lock-in)
  • PPF: If you want safety and guaranteed returns
  • Term Insurance: ₹25,000-30,000 premium (essential protection)

Income: ₹8-15 Lakh

  • ELSS: ₹1,00,000 (higher growth potential)
  • PPF: ₹50,000 (stable, tax-free)
  • NPS (80CCD 1B): ₹50,000 (additional deduction)
  • Home loan principal: If you have a home loan

Income: Above ₹15 Lakh

  • Maximize all deductions: 80C (₹1.5L) + 80CCD (₹50K) + 80D (₹50K) = ₹2.5 lakh savings
  • Home loan + HRA: Combined savings of ₹3-4 lakh
  • Consider old regime: With max deductions, old regime often saves more tax
Tax Saving Math
At 30% tax bracket, every ₹1,50,000 in 80C saves you ₹45,000 in tax (plus cess). Add NPS ₹50K (₹15,000 saving) and 80D ₹50K (₹15,000 saving). Total: ₹75,000+ saved annually!

Common 80C Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Buying insurance for tax saving: Endowment plans give 4-6% returns. Buy term insurance + invest in ELSS instead.
  2. Not using 80CCD(1B): The additional ₹50,000 NPS deduction is separate from 80C. Most people miss it!
  3. Ignoring tuition fees: Children's school fees qualify for 80C. Keep receipts!
  4. Investing in March: Don't panic-buy in March. Plan investments at the start of the financial year.
  5. Not tracking home loan principal: Your loan statement shows principal repayment. Claim it every year.
