GSTR-3B Filing Complete Guide 2026 — Step by Step Process

January 19, 20268 min readBy ReconcileAI Team

GSTR-3B is the summary monthly return that every regular GST-registered taxpayer must file. It includes details of outward supplies, input tax credit claimed, and tax payment. This guide covers the complete GSTR-3B filing process for FY 2026-27.

What is GSTR-3B?

GSTR-3B is a self-declared summary return filed monthly (or quarterly under QRMP). Unlike GSTR-1 (which is invoice-wise), GSTR-3B contains consolidated totals of:

  • Outward taxable supplies (sales)
  • Inward supplies liable to reverse charge
  • Input tax credit (ITC) claimed
  • Exempt and nil-rated supplies
  • Interest and late fee payable
  • Tax payment (cash + ITC utilization)

Interest: 18% per annum on net tax payable, calculated from the due date.

Step-by-Step GSTR-3B Filing Process

Step 1: Reconcile Before Filing

Before filing GSTR-3B, reconcile your books with GSTR-2B. This ensures:

  • ITC claimed matches GSTR-2B
  • Outward supplies match GSTR-1
  • No missing or duplicate entries

Important: Since Rule 36(4), ITC is restricted to what appears in GSTR-2B. Unmatched ITC will be reversed.

Step 2: Log In and Select Period

Visit gst.gov.in. Navigate to Services > Returns > Returns Dashboard. Select the tax period and click GSTR-3B.

Step 3: Fill Table 3.1 — Outward Supplies

Step 5: Fill Table 5 — Exempt and Nil Supplies

Report values of exempt, nil-rated, and non-GST inward supplies. This includes agricultural produce, certain healthcare services, and educational services.

Step 6: Fill Table 6 — Payment of Tax

The system auto-computes tax payable based on Tables 3-5. Verify:

  • IGST payable: Inter-state supplies
  • CGST + SGST payable: Intra-state supplies
  • ITC utilization: Auto-optimized (IGST first, then CGST/SGST)
  • Cash payment: Any remaining liability after ITC adjustment

Step 7: Verify & File

Generate GSTR-3B summary. Cross-check all figures. File with DSC or EVC. Payment can be made through electronic cash ledger or credit ledger.

Best Practice
File GSTR-3B by the 18th of each month (not 20th) to avoid last-minute portal issues. Also, ensure your electronic cash/credit ledger has sufficient balance before filing to avoid payment failures.

ITC Utilization Rules (Order of Set-Off)

  1. IGST credit: First utilized against IGST liability, then CGST, then SGST
  2. CGST credit: Utilized against CGST liability, then IGST (NOT SGST)
  3. SGST credit: Utilized against SGST liability, then IGST (NOT CGST)

Important: CGST credit cannot be set off against SGST liability and vice versa. Only IGST credit can be used across all taxes.

QRMP Scheme Payment (IFF + Monthly Payment)

If you are under the QRMP scheme:

  • IFF (Invoice Furnishing Facility): Upload B2B invoices monthly (optional, by 13th)
  • Monthly tax payment: Pay tax monthly using PMT-06 (by 25th)
  • Quarterly GSTR-3B: File summary return quarterly (22nd/24th)

Common GSTR-3B Filing Errors

  1. ITC claimed beyond GSTR-2B: Restricted under Rule 36(4). Always match with 2B first.
  2. Wrong tax rates: Verify applicable GST rates for each product/service category.
  3. Missing reverse charge: Report RCM supplies in Table 3.1(d) and claim ITC in Table 4(A)(3).
  4. Credit note not netted: Credit notes must reduce outward supply values in Table 3.1.
  5. Cash ledger shortfall: Ensure sufficient balance. Failed payment = late fee + interest.
