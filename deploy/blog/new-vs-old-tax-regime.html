New vs Old Tax Regime 2026-27 — Which Saves More Tax? (With Examples)

January 17, 20267 min readBy ReconcileAI Team

Choosing between the new and old tax regime can save or cost you thousands of rupees every year. The answer depends on your income, deductions, and financial situation. This guide gives you the complete comparison with real numbers so you can make the right choice for FY 2026-27.

Key Takeaway
The break-even point depends on your deductions. If total deductions exceed ₹4,00,000, the old regime usually wins. Below that, the new regime is typically better. Always calculate both!

Decision Framework

  1. List all your deductions: 80C, 80D, HRA, home loan, NPS, education loan, donations.
  2. If total deductions > ₹4 lakh: Old regime likely better.
  3. If total deductions < ₹2.5 lakh: New regime almost always better.
  4. If deductions are between ₹2.5-4 lakh: Calculate both — it depends on income level.
  5. Use a calculator: Don't guess. Our free calculator shows exact numbers.
Pro Tip for Businesses
If you are a business owner or freelancer, the old regime allows you to deduct business expenses, depreciation, and professional tax. This can significantly reduce taxable income. However, once you choose the new regime, switching back to old is allowed only ONCE in your lifetime.
