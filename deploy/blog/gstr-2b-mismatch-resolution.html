GSTR-2B Mismatch Resolution — How to Fix ITC Discrepancies

January 10, 20267 min readBy ReconcileAI Team

If your books show more ITC than GSTR-2B, or your GSTR-2B has invoices you don't recognize, you have a mismatch. This guide covers exactly how to identify, categorize, and resolve every type of GSTR-2B mismatch.

Types of GSTR-2B Mismatches

Mismatch TypeWhat It MeansRoot Cause
Invoice in books, not in 2BYou claimed ITC but supplier hasn't filedSupplier default
Invoice in 2B, not in booksSupplier filed but you haven't recordedMissing purchase entry
Tax amount mismatchSame invoice, different tax amountsWrong tax rate, rounding, amendments
Duplicate in 2BSame invoice appears twiceSupplier filed duplicate GSTR-1
Amended invoiceOriginal + amended both in 2BSupplier amended GSTR-1

Step-by-Step Resolution Process

Step 1: Categorize All Mismatches

Sort every mismatched invoice into one of the categories above. This determines the resolution action.

Step 2: Missing Supplier Filings

For invoices in your books but not in GSTR-2B:

  1. Contact the supplier immediately
  2. Ask them to file GSTR-1 (or amend if already filed with wrong details)
  3. Set a follow-up reminder (7 days)
  4. If supplier doesn't respond, do not claim ITC until 2B shows the invoice
ITC Rule 36(4)
You can only claim ITC that appears in GSTR-2B. If the supplier hasn't filed, the ITC is not available this month. Claim it in the month it appears in 2B. Do not risk claiming unmatched ITC — it will be reversed and you'll pay interest.

Step 3: Missing Purchase Entries

For invoices in GSTR-2B but not in your books:

  1. Check if the invoice was received but not recorded
  2. Check if the invoice is from a different entity (wrong GSTIN mapping)
  3. Record the missing purchase entry in your books
  4. Claim the ITC in GSTR-3B

Step 4: Tax Amount Mismatches

When the same invoice has different amounts in books vs 2B:

  • Small differences (< ₹1): Rounding adjustment. Acceptable.
  • Tax rate difference: Verify the correct HSN code and rate. Ask supplier to amend.
  • Valuation difference: Check if there are additional charges (freight, insurance) included.
  • Amendment difference: Supplier amended the invoice. Match with amended version.

Step 5: Handle Credit Notes

Credit notes in GSTR-2B reduce your ITC. Make sure:

  • Every credit note is recorded in your purchase returns
  • Debit notes are matched against original invoices
  • Net ITC position is calculated correctly

Preventing Future Mismatches

  1. Monthly reconciliation: Don't wait until year-end. Reconcile every month.
  2. Supplier scorecard: Track which suppliers consistently fail to file on time.
  3. Invoice verification: Record invoices immediately when received.
  4. Use automated tools: Manual matching is error-prone. Automation catches what humans miss.
Best Practice
Download GSTR-2B on the 14th (when it's generated), reconcile by the 20th, and follow up with suppliers by the 25th. This gives enough buffer before GSTR-3B filing (20th of next month).
