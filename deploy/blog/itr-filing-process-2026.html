ITR Filing Process Step by Step 2026 — Complete Guide for India

January 14, 20268 min readBy ReconcileAI Team

Filing your Income Tax Return (ITR) is mandatory for most taxpayers in India. Whether you are a salaried employee, freelancer, or business owner, this guide covers the complete ITR filing process for FY 2026-27 (AY 2027-28) step by step.

Who Needs to File ITR?

  • Income above basic exemption: ₹2.5 lakh (old regime) / ₹3 lakh (new regime)
  • TDS deducted: Even if total income is below exemption, file ITR to claim refund
  • Foreign income or assets: Mandatory filing regardless of income level
  • Business turnover above ₹60 lakh: Mandatory (even if income is below exemption)
  • Professional receipts above ₹10 lakh: Mandatory filing
  • Electricity bill above ₹1 lakh: Mandatory filing
  • Deposits above ₹1 crore in bank: Mandatory filing

Step-by-Step ITR Filing Process

Step 1: Gather Documents

  • Form 16 (from employer) or Form 26AS
  • Interest certificates (banks, post office)
  • Capital gains statements (mutual funds, stocks, property)
  • Rent receipts (if claiming HRA)
  • Investment proofs (80C, 80D, NPS, etc.)
  • Bank account details (for refund)
  • AIS (Annual Information Statement) from Income Tax portal

Step 2: Log In to Income Tax Portal

Visit incometax.gov.in and log in with your PAN and password. If you don't have an account, register using your PAN.

Step 3: Verify Form 26AS and AIS

Download Form 26AS and AIS. Check that all TDS credits are reflected. Compare with your Form 16 or TDS certificates. Any mismatch must be resolved before filing.

Step 4: Select ITR Form & Assessment Year

Select the correct ITR form (ITR-1 for most salaried). Choose AY 2027-28 for FY 2026-27 income.

Step 5: Fill Income Details

  1. Salary income: Auto-filled from Form 16 in most cases
  2. House property: Rental income and home loan interest
  3. Business/Profession: P&L and balance sheet (ITR-3/4)
  4. Capital gains: Sale of property, shares, mutual funds
  5. Other sources: Interest, dividends, FD interest

Step 6: Claim Deductions

Enter all eligible deductions:

  • Section 80C: PPF, ELSS, LIC, EPF, tuition fees (up to ₹1.5 lakh)
  • Section 80D: Health insurance premium (up to ₹25,000/₹50,000)
  • Section 80CCD(1B): NPS additional contribution (up to ₹50,000)
  • Section 24(b): Home loan interest (up to ₹2 lakh)
  • Section 80E: Education loan interest (no limit)
  • Section 80TTA/TTB: Savings interest deduction (₹10,000/₹50,000)

Need help with 80C? Read our complete Section 80C guide.

Step 7: Verify Tax Computation

Check the auto-computed tax liability. Ensure TDS credit is properly adjusted. Verify the refund or payable amount.

Step 8: File & Verify ITR

Submit the return and complete verification within 30 days using:

  • Aadhaar OTP (most common)
  • Net banking
  • DSC (Digital Signature)
  • Physical ITR-V to CPC Bengaluru (last resort)
Unverified ITR = Invalid
If you don't verify within 30 days, your ITR is treated as not filed. You'll have to file again. Always complete verification immediately after submission.

ITR Filing Tips to Avoid Notices

  1. Match AIS with ITR: All income reported in AIS must be declared in ITR.
  2. Report all bank interest: Even ₹500 interest must be declared.
  3. Capital gains reporting: Every buy-sell transaction must be reported.
  4. Don't claim fake deductions: 80C/80D claims without proof invite scrutiny.
  5. File on time: Late filing means losing the option to carry forward losses.
