GST Registration Process Online 2026 — Step by Step Guide

January 20, 20268 min readBy ReconcileAI Team

GST registration is mandatory for most businesses in India with turnover above the prescribed threshold. Whether you are starting a new business, an e-commerce seller, or a service provider, this guide covers the complete GST registration process online for 2026.

Documents Required for GST Registration

For Proprietorship

  • PAN card of proprietor
  • Aadhaar card of proprietor
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Business address proof (rent agreement / electricity bill / property tax receipt)
  • Bank account details (cancelled cheque or bank statement)
  • Digital Signature Certificate (optional for proprietorship)

For Partnership / LLP

  • PAN card of firm
  • Partnership deed / LLP agreement
  • PAN and Aadhaar of all partners
  • Photographs of all partners
  • Address proof of business
  • Bank account details
  • Registration certificate (if any)

For Private Limited Company

  • Certificate of Incorporation
  • MOA and AOA
  • PAN card of company
  • PAN and Aadhaar of directors
  • Photographs of directors
  • Board resolution for authorized signatory
  • Address proof of registered office
  • Bank account details
Pro Tip
Keep all documents in PDF format before starting the application. The GST portal accepts PDFs up to 1MB. If documents are larger, compress them. Having everything ready reduces registration time from 2 weeks to 3-5 days.

Step-by-Step GST Registration Process

Step 1: Visit GST Portal

Go to www.gst.gov.in. Click Services > Registration > New Registration.

Step 2: Fill Part A of GST REG-01

  • Select: "Taxpayer" in "I am a" dropdown
  • State and District
  • Legal name of business (as per PAN)
  • PAN number
  • Email address and mobile number (for OTP)
  • Enter captcha and click "Proceed"

Step 3: Verify OTP

You will receive OTP on email and mobile. Enter both OTPs. The system generates a Temporary Reference Number (TRN). Save this TRN — you need it to continue the application.

Step 4: Fill Part B (Within 15 Days)

Log in with your TRN. Complete the remaining details:

  1. Business details: Trade name, constitution, date of commencement, reason for registration
  2. Promoter/Partners: Personal details, Aadhaar verification
  3. Authorized signatory: Name, PAN, mobile, email
  4. Business place: Address, nature of possession (owned/rented/consented), electricity bill
  5. Goods and services: HSN/SAC codes (up to 5 goods + 5 services for regular, up to 10 for others)
  6. Bank account: Account number, IFSC, bank name
  7. Verification: Declaration and sign

Step 5: Aadhaar Authentication

Complete Aadhaar authentication for faster processing. If Aadhaar verification is done, registration is typically granted in 3-5 working days. Without Aadhaar, it may take up to 30 days (physical verification).

Step 6: Submit & Get ARN

Submit the application with DSC or EVC. You receive an Application Reference Number (ARN). Track your application status using ARN on the GST portal.

Step 7: Receive GSTIN

After approval, you receive:

  • GSTIN (15-digit GST Identification Number)
  • Registration Certificate (Form GST REG-06)
  • Access to file returns on the portal
GSTIN Structure
Your 15-digit GSTIN: 22AAAAA0000A1Z5
Digits 1-2: State code | Digits 3-12: PAN | Digit 13: Entity number | Digit 14: Default "Z" | Digit 15: Check digit
Example: 27AAECS1234F1Z5 = Maharashtra + PAN AAECS1234F + Entity 1 + Z + Check digit 5

Common GST Registration Mistakes

  1. Wrong business name: Must exactly match PAN records. Any mismatch causes rejection.
  2. Wrong HSN/SAC codes: Select accurate codes for your products/services. Wrong codes affect return filing.
  3. Missing address proof: Ensure address proof is recent (within 2 months). Old bills cause queries.
  4. Not completing Aadhaar: Without Aadhaar, physical verification is slow. Always opt for Aadhaar authentication.
  5. Ignoring queries: If the officer raises a query (REG-03), respond within 7 days. Non-response = rejection.
After Registration
Once you get GSTIN, you must:
✓ Display GSTIN on your business premises and invoices
✓ File GSTR-1 (monthly/quarterly) and GSTR-3B (monthly)
✓ Maintain proper books of accounts
✓ Issue GST-compliant tax invoices
✓ Reconcile books with GSTR-2B monthly (read our GST reconciliation guide)
