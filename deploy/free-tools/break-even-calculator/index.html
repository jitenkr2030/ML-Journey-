FAQ

What is the break-even point?
The break-even point (BEP) is the level of sales where total revenue equals total costs — no profit, no loss. Below BEP = loss. Above BEP = profit. BEP (Units) = Fixed Costs / Contribution per Unit.
What is contribution margin?
Contribution per unit = Selling Price − Variable Cost. It's the amount each unit contributes toward covering fixed costs. After fixed costs are covered, every additional unit's contribution is pure profit.
How can I lower my break-even point?
Three ways: (1) Reduce fixed costs (lower rent, salaries), (2) Increase selling price, (3) Reduce variable cost per unit (cheaper materials, better supplier). Even small changes can significantly lower BEP.
