FAQ

Are these live exchange rates?
These are approximate reference rates. Actual rates vary by bank, forex service (Thomas Cook, BookMyForex), and transaction type. For business transactions, always confirm with your bank.
What is the difference between buy rate and sell rate?
The buy rate is what the bank pays you when you sell foreign currency. The sell rate is what you pay when buying foreign currency. The bank's margin (spread) is typically 1-3%.
Which currencies are supported?
