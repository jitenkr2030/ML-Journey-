Advance Tax — FAQ

Who needs to pay advance tax?
Anyone whose estimated tax liability exceeds ₹10,000 after TDS. Includes business owners, professionals, freelancers, and those with capital gains or rental income. Salaried employees with only salary income don't need to pay separately.
What is interest u/s 234B and 234C?
234B: 1% per month if 90% of tax not paid by 31st March.
234C: 1% per month for each quarter's shortfall (15%/45%/75%/100% cumulative).
What about capital gains in Q4?
For capital gains in the last quarter (Jan-Mar), pay tax on that gain in the Q4 installment (by 15 March) without interest. Calculate other income's advance tax normally.
