TDS Calculator — FAQ

What is TDS and when is it deducted?
TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) is income tax deducted at the time of payment by the payer. The deductor must deposit the TDS with the government by the 7th of the following month. TDS is deducted when payment exceeds the specified threshold for that section.
What happens if PAN is not provided?
If the payee does not provide PAN, TDS is deducted at 20% or the applicable rate, whichever is higher, as per Section 206AA. Additionally, Section 206AB mandates higher TDS (5%) for non-filers of income tax returns for the last 2 years.
What is the difference between 194C and 194J?
194C (Contractor): For work contracts — construction, supply of labor, manufacturing. Rate: 1% (individual/HUF) or 2% (company).

194J (Professional): For professional/technical services — CA, doctor, lawyer, engineer, IT consultant. Rate: 10% (or 2% for technical services/royalty).
