GSTR-1 Filing Process Step by Step — Complete Guide 2026

January 16, 20268 min readBy ReconcileAI Team

GSTR-1 is the monthly or quarterly return that every registered taxpayer under GST must file. It contains details of all outward supplies (sales). This guide walks you through the complete GSTR-1 filing process step by step for FY 2026-27.

What is GSTR-1?

GSTR-1 is a return of outward supplies (sales) that must be filed by every regular GST-registered taxpayer. It captures details of:

  • B2B invoices (sales to registered businesses)
  • B2C large invoices (sales above ₹2.5 lakh to unregistered)
  • B2C small invoices (sales below ₹2.5 lakh to unregistered)
  • Export invoices (with/without payment of IGST)
  • Credit notes and debit notes
  • Amendments to previous returns

Step-by-Step GSTR-1 Filing Process

Step 1: Prepare Your Sales Data

Before filing, compile all sales data for the tax period:

  • Invoice-wise B2B sales (with buyer GSTIN)
  • B2C sales summary
  • Export sales
  • Credit/debit notes
  • Nil-rated and exempt supplies

Tip: Use accounting software (Tally, Busy, Zoho Books) to auto-generate this data. Manual compilation is error-prone.

Step 2: Log In to GST Portal

Visit gst.gov.in and log in with your credentials. Navigate to Services > Returns > Returns Dashboard.

Step 3: Select Tax Period

Choose the financial year and month/quarter for which you are filing. Click Search.

Step 4: Fill GSTR-1 Form

GSTR-1 has multiple sections (tables). Fill each one:

TableDescriptionMandatory?
4A, 4B, 4CB2B invoices (registered buyers)If applicable
5B2C large invoices (> ₹2.5 lakh)If applicable
6Exports (with/without IGST)If applicable
7B2C small sales (summary)If applicable
8, 9, 10Nil-rated, exempt, non-GST suppliesIf applicable
9BCredit / Debit notesIf applicable
11AAmendments to B2B invoicesIf applicable
12HSN-wise summary of outward suppliesMandatory

Step 5: Verify & Generate Summary

Click Generate GSTR-1 Summary. Verify the totals match your books. Check for any errors flagged by the portal.

Step 6: File With DSC or EVC

Once verified, file GSTR-1 using:

  • DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) — for companies and LLPs
  • EVC (Electronic Verification Code) — OTP-based, for proprietorships
Best Practice
File GSTR-1 by the 8th of each month (not 11th) to allow buffer time for corrections. Also ensure your buyer can see your invoices in their GSTR-2B before their filing deadline (20th).

Common GSTR-1 Filing Errors

  1. Wrong GSTIN of buyer: Verify buyer GSTIN before entering. Invalid GSTIN = buyer can't claim ITC.
  2. Missing invoices: Every B2B invoice MUST be reported. Missing entries create ITC mismatch for your buyer.
  3. Wrong HSN codes: Incorrect HSN affects GST rate and buyer's ITC. Use the official HSN code list.
  4. Not reporting credit notes: Credit notes must be reported in Table 9B. They reduce your tax liability.
  5. Amendment delays: Invoices can only be amended in the next GSTR-1 (not retrospectively after 2 periods).

QRMP Scheme (Quarterly Filing)

Businesses with turnover up to ₹5 crore can opt for the Quarterly Return Monthly Payment (QRMP) scheme:

  • GSTR-1: Filed quarterly (13th of month after quarter)
  • GSTR-3B: Filed quarterly, but tax paid monthly
  • IFF (Invoice Furnishing Facility): Optional monthly invoice upload for B2B sales

Opt-in window: 1st to 30th of the preceding quarter. Opt-out by changing filing frequency on the portal.
