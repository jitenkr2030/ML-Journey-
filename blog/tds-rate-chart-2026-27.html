No PAN? Higher Rate Applies!
If the deductee does not provide PAN, TDS is deducted at 20% or the rate specified in the Act, whichever is higher. Under Section 206AA, this higher rate applies universally. Always collect PAN before making payments.

Key Changes in Budget 2026

  • Section 194BA: TDS on online gaming winnings at 30% with no threshold. Winnings are net of losses across gaming platforms.
  • Section 194N: Cash withdrawal TDS continues at 2% above ₹1 crore. For non-filers, 2% above ₹20 lakh.
  • Section 194R: Perquisite valuation clarified. GST component is included in the perquisite value.
  • Section 194Q: Purchase of goods TDS at 0.1% continues. Applies to buyers with turnover exceeding ₹10 crore.

Pro Tip
For TDS deducted in March, the deposit due date is 30th April (not 7th April). This gives you extra time to compile March payments. But for all other months, the 7th deadline is strict — late payment attracts interest at 1% per month under Section 201(1A).

How to Calculate TDS Correctly

  1. Identify the section based on nature of payment
  2. Check the threshold — if payment is below threshold, no TDS
  3. Verify PAN — without PAN, rate doubles (or 20%, whichever is higher)
  4. Apply surcharge/cess if deductee is non-resident (Section 195)
  5. Deduct TDS at the time of credit or payment, whichever is earlier
  6. Deposit by 7th of the following month
  7. File quarterly TDS returns (Form 24Q, 26Q, 27Q)
