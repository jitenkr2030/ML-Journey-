GST Reconciliation Process Step by Step — Complete Guide 2026

January 15, 20268 min readBy ReconcileAI Team

GST reconciliation is one of the most critical compliance activities for every Indian business. If your books don't match your GSTR-2B, you could lose Input Tax Credit (ITC), face notices, or pay unnecessary interest. This guide walks you through the complete process step by step.

What is GST Reconciliation?

GST reconciliation is the process of matching your accounting books (purchase register) with the GSTR-2B (auto-populated ITC statement) on the GST portal. The goal is to ensure that every input tax credit you claim actually appears in your supplier's filed returns.

Why It Matters
If you claim ITC that is not reflected in GSTR-2B, it will be reversed. Additionally, Rule 36(4) restricts ITC to what is visible in GSTR-2B. Unmatched credits = lost money.

Step-by-Step GST Reconciliation Process

Step 1: Download GSTR-2B

Log in to the GST portal, navigate to Services > Returns Dashboard > GSTR-2B, and download the statement in JSON or Excel format. GSTR-2B is auto-generated on the 14th of every month based on your suppliers' filings.

Step 2: Export Your Purchase Register

From your accounting software (Tally, Busy, Zoho Books, or manual records), export your purchase register for the same tax period. You need:

  • GSTIN of supplier
  • Invoice number and date
  • Taxable value
  • CGST, SGST, IGST amounts
  • Invoice type (B2B, B2C, credit note, debit note)

Step 3: Match Records

Compare each record in your purchase register with GSTR-2B. Records will fall into three categories:

CategoryDescriptionAction
MatchedInvoice present in both books and GSTR-2B with same amountsClaim full ITC
In books, not in 2BYou recorded it, but supplier hasn't filedFollow up with supplier
In 2B, not in booksSupplier filed, but you haven't recordedRecord the purchase entry
MismatchPresent in both but amounts differInvestigate and correct

Step 4: Resolve Mismatches

For each mismatch, identify the root cause:

  • Missing invoices: Supplier has not filed GSTR-1. Contact supplier and request filing.
  • Wrong GSTIN: Invoice uploaded under wrong GSTIN. Ask supplier to amend.
  • Amount differences: Could be rounding errors, wrong tax rate, or partial credit notes.
  • Duplicate entries: Same invoice recorded twice in your books. Remove duplicate.
  • Debit/Credit notes: Ensure you've matched the net position, not just invoices.
Important Deadline
GSTR-2B is generated monthly. ITC must be claimed in the correct period. Late claims may require GSTR-3B amendments. Section 16(4) restricts ITC claims to November of the following financial year (or annual return filing date, whichever is earlier).

Step 5: Update Your Books

After matching, update your purchase register:

  1. Add entries found in GSTR-2B but missing in your books
  2. Flag entries in your books but not in GSTR-2B (don't claim ITC yet)
  3. Correct amount mismatches
  4. Record the final ITC eligible amount

Step 6: File GSTR-3B

Use the reconciled ITC amount in your GSTR-3B filing. The ITC claimed should match the eligible amount from GSTR-2B (after adjustments).

Common GST Reconciliation Mistakes

  1. Not reconciling monthly: Monthly reconciliation prevents year-end surprises and interest.
  2. Claiming ITC without verification: Always verify against GSTR-2B before claiming.
  3. Ignoring credit notes: Credit notes reduce ITC. Must be netted against purchases.
  4. Not following up with suppliers: Missing supplier filings = lost ITC. Proactive follow-up is essential.
  5. Rounding differences: Small rounding differences (under Rs. 1) can be adjusted. Larger ones need investigation.
